Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information the mobile game Arcane Fusion: Merge & Defend ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by [DEVELOPER NAME]. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cards, levels, gems, settings, player name and avatar) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Cloud save and leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game stores a copy of your progress in your Google account (Play Games "Saved Games") so you can continue on a new phone, and submits your best scores to leaderboards. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Your Play Games profile name may be visible on leaderboards. You can delete cloud saves and leaderboard data from your Google Play Games settings.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google tells the game which items you bought so it can unlock them.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one for a bonus). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate an approximate location), device information and ad interaction data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell your personal information. The player name you type is only shown inside the game.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data security and deletion

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. To delete your data: uninstall the game (device data), delete the game's data in Google Play Games settings (cloud save and leaderboards), and reset your advertising ID in Android settings. You can also contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] with any request.
